I am a Predoctoral Fellow at the [Stanford Institute for Excellence in Survey Research (SIESR)](https://iriss.stanford.edu/siesr) and in the [Department of Sociology](https://sociology.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University.

My research leverages survey data to measure political attitudes across racial/ethnic and underrepresented groups. I am particularly focused on Asian American and Pacific Islander (AAPI) communities, with previous data fellowships at several national AAPI nonprofits.

I received my B.A. from Yale University in 2024, with majors in Ethnicity, Race & Migration and Statistics & Data Science. I grew up in Chicago.
